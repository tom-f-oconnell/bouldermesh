Details

Meshes each area's boulder problems in its order until you stop it, keeping every mesh finished before then. Each can take most of an hour.

Before a climb is meshed, the page of its lowest Mountain Project area is read, for whether that's one rock. Where its name or description says so ("Drifter/Smoking Boulder"), the area's climbs on it are harvested, wherever they come in the order, and meshed together from all their photos: one mesh, counting once toward a limit. It shows what their photos do, often not all the way round, and is called a whole boulder only when it places every climb on it. A climb it hardly shows is meshed on its own too. (The admin page can have every climb meshed on its own instead.)

Where the area holds several rocks ("Stained Glass/Flyboy Area", "Birthday Boulders"), or its page doesn't say, each climb is meshed on its own, counting one each. Once its climbs within the limit are through, those whose photos show the same rock are meshed together as well: clusters of a boulder's climbs, not always all of them. These, like the climbs a boulder's mesh hardly shows, come on top of the limit.

Several areas, one a line, are taken in the order given, top first, after any running already; one with no climb to take for now (waiting for volunteers) is passed over until it has. An area listed already is taken up again with these settings, keeping what's done, and moves to its place in the new list. One removed and entered again starts over, but its climbs that have meshes keep them.
