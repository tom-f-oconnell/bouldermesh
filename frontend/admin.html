Areas

Together: the climbs on one rock are harvested, then meshed from all their photos as one mesh, which shows each from where its own photos were taken; a climb it hardly shows is meshed on its own too. Where the page names several rocks (a sector, "… Area", "… Boulders") or doesn't say, each climb is meshed on its own, and once they're through, those whose photos show the same rock are meshed together as well. A climb alone on its rock is meshed on its own either way.
Most of a harvest's time on a small server. Pairs matched before are kept, for each way, so a climb harvested again only matches what's new. On 12 of hal's harvests FLANN kept 92 of the 101 search photos brute force matched (and one it didn't), and more checks didn't close the gap, so brute force stays the default.
A boulder's mesh tries the photos it leaves out again against itself. Of what's still left, each set COLMAP places together (6 photos or more) is a scene to mesh apart, as another side or rock, if it's tied to the boulder: it holds a climb's Mountain Project page photo, or 2 of its photos match photos the mesh placed (16 verified matches, as the off-topic filter takes one). Its card says boulder only when no such scene waits or made a mesh (one tried that made nothing doesn't count), and every climb on it was harvested and tried in it. This queues each never tried, or cut short. Meshes from before those were recorded go by the climbs with 6 or more photos left out, and stay groups until meshed again (the button above, for all of them, or a climb's card's Mesh the boulder again), which checks what they left out; nothing meshes those left-out photos as they are, as for Drifter that made a mesh of another rock. Meshing again uses the photos as harvested (only problems on a boulder's page never harvested are), and first reads what each one's Mountain Project area is, where that never was: one whose page doesn't say it's one rock isn't meshed whole again, as its climbs are meshed on their own and pooled by their photos.

Compute

Each worker takes one area's climb at a time, and harvests it here, off-topic filter and all. this server then meshes it here (with reconstructions above 0), and also runs the jobs asked for on this page; each remote hands its climbs to a machine, one at a time: Climbs at once meshed on other machines is how many of those workers there are, so how many climbs can be on their way at once (one harvested here while another is meshed), even with one machine. One idle has no climb it can take now: the rest are waiting for volunteers' videos, for others on their boulder, or for each other. Cores for this server's own processing keeps harvests, the filter, packing meshes and finding beta here to that many of its cores (the last ones), leaving the first for visitors: all by default. Whatever it is, this server filters one harvest's photos at a time, and the public setup holds its native libraries to one thread each (OMP_NUM_THREADS and the like in docker-compose.public.yml), as two filters at once spun in the kernel for 5 hours on Nov 12, 2026.

How long meshes take

Medians, by what made them and how many photos went in. Meshing: the reconstruction's tools, as long as they ran; CPU: the CPU time they used; start to finish: from starting it to the mesh saved, with any wait for a machine and sending photos and results; waited: for a machine to take it.
